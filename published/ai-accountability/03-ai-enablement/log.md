# Change log — AI Enablement

## 2026-09-13

* **Update**: [Finding Where AI Earns Its Keep](/ai-accountability/03-ai-enablement/04-finding-where-ai-earns-its-keep.md) no longer calls
  adding AI to an existing process "the biggest mistake". It now says a well-fitted tool in the current
  process can pay, that gains tend to be larger when the work changes along with the tool, and links to the
  evidence in [Rethink the Process, or Just Add AI?](/research-notes/rethink-the-process-or-just-add-ai.md).
