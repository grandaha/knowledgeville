Plain-language definitions of the terms these notes use. It grows as the notes do.

**Research note.** A page recording the current understanding of an open question. Its findings
are sourced and verified; its conclusion is provisional and expected to change.

**Graduated.** A note whose question has settled, rewritten as a full page in the bundle where it
belongs. What stays here is a pointer.

**Superseded.** A note whose conclusion turned out to be wrong, kept in place and marked, with a
link to what replaced it.

**Open question.** A question a note names and deliberately does not answer, because the evidence
to answer it does not exist yet or was not found. The most useful part of most notes.
