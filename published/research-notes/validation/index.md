# Validation

A lighter process does not mean a lighter factual standard. What these notes hold loosely is
their conclusions, never their facts.

**What is checked, on every note:**

- Every number traces to a primary source in this bundle's evidence ledger, enforced
  automatically before a note can ship.
- Every stated fact is verified against that primary source, not against recall.
- A citation audit runs on any note that cites anything, checking for misquotes,
  misattributions, and figures that do not add up.

**What is deliberately not run:** the adversarial review panel that argues finished bundles into
shape. That check asks whether a bundle's conclusions hold together well enough to be relied on
— which is the wrong question for a page whose whole premise is that its conclusion is not
settled yet. It runs when a note graduates into a real bundle.

**Where uncertainty shows up:** in the notes themselves, named rather than hidden. A note that
cannot substantiate something says so and states what would settle it.

Dated audits land in `audits/` as they are run.
